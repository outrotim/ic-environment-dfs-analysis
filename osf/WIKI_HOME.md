# Study 17 research archive

This OSF project preserves the analytic materials for a harmonised longitudinal
study of intrinsic capacity, environmental support, and disability-free
survival across HRS, ELSA, CHARLS, SHARE, and MHAS, with KLoSA used for
reduced-indicator qualitative replication.

## Transparency statement

This is a **retrospective research archive, not a pre-registration**. The
statistical analysis plan is dated 11 March 2026 and was maintained locally
before substantive outcome modelling, but it was not publicly posted before
analysis. The archive documents both planned analyses and decisions made during
analysis; it is not presented as prospective registration.

## Contents

- `code/`: 19 numbered R scripts.
- `tests/`: regression tests for sample construction and principal derived estimates.
- `docs/`: SAP, study schema, harmonisation map, data inventory, and timeline.
- `results/`: aggregate cohort-level and pooled result files.
- `figures/`: reproducible PDF figure outputs.

## Data access

The source cohort data remain with their repositories and are not redistributed.
Researchers must register separately with HRS, ELSA, CHARLS, SHARE, MHAS, KLoSA,
and the Gateway to Global Aging Data and comply with each data-use agreement.

## Interpretation boundary

The multiplicative interaction describes relative-scale association. Additive
interaction was not detected; absolute risk differences were similar across IC
strata; cohort estimates were heterogeneous; and KLoSA's confidence interval
crossed 1. The archive therefore supports reproducibility, not a causal policy
claim.
